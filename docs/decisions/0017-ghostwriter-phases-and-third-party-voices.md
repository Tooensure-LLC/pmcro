# 0017: Ghostwriter phases; third-party voices only under contract

Status: accepted (candidate) · 2026-10-01 (owner idea: "Ghost Writer" starts with scripts, later songs, possibly using other people's voices as revenue grows). Extends ADR 0008, which allowed only the founder's own voice.

## Decision
Ghostwriter grows in three phases (`docs/product/ghostwriter.md`). Phase 1 exists. Phase 2 (lyrics and songs as text) waits for a revenue threshold the founder sets. Phase 3 (voice) additionally requires: a signed consent and licence from each voice owner with scope, term, compensation and revocation; local training and storage; plain disclosure in each published piece; human approval of every release; and a lawyer's review. Impersonation of any real person, and any use of a voice without a signed licence, stay forbidden. Until phase 3 conditions are met, ADR 0008 stands unchanged: the founder's own voice only.

## Consequences
The idea can proceed in order without committing to something the law or platforms may forbid. No voice or music tooling is chosen or tested. Contract terms, laws and platform rules are OPEN and not legal advice. Economic frames track cost and revenue per piece.
