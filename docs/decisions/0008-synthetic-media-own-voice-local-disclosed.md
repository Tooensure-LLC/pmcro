# 0008: Synthetic media: own voice only, trained locally, always disclosed

Status: accepted (candidate) · 2026-10-01 (owner idea: train the founder's voice for content)

## Context
The company wants a content plugin that writes scripts and could later voice them in the founder's own trained voice, and make images. Synthetic voice and likeness can deceive and can be abused.

## Decision
Only the founder's own voice and face, after a written consent record kept in the private tier. Recordings and trained models stay on the company's own machine and are never committed or uploaded. Published synthetic content says so in the content itself. No other person's voice or likeness is in scope. Scripts must carry sourced claims; the checker enforces structure (claims have sources, disclosure present when synthetic, no private-tier markers) and never issues Checker verdicts.

## Consequences
Voice and image tools, models and licenses are OPEN and untested. The checker cannot verify truth, so a human or the Checker role still reviews claims. Platform and legal rules vary and change; check them before publishing.
