# Ghostwriter (product line)

Status: PROPOSED. Owner idea, 2026-10-01: once the company earns more it writes songs and more, possibly using other people's voices; for now it writes content scripts (for example YouTube). Only phase 1 exists.

## Phases

| Phase | What | State | Gate to start |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Scripts | Video, podcast and short-form scripts in the founder's own voice, with sourced claims and disclosure | Built and tested for structure: `pmcro-content` / `content-script` | none |
| 2. Lyrics and songs (text) | Original lyrics and song structures for the founder or for clients who commission and approve them | Not built | a revenue threshold set by the founder (OPEN: the number is human-owned) |
| 3. Voice | Synthetic singing or speaking voice | Not built | the conditions in ADR 0017, and a lawyer's review |

## What "ghostwriting" means here

Writing for a named person who knows, approves and publishes under their own name is ordinary ghostwriting. It becomes deception when the audience is led to believe something false: a synthetic voice presented as a live person, or a person's voice or likeness used without their agreement. The company does the first and refuses the second.

## Third-party voices (phase 3 only, ADR 0017)

Allowed only with all of: a written consent and licence from the voice owner (scope, term, compensation, how to revoke it); training and storage on the company's own machine; a plain disclosure in every published piece; human approval of each release; a lawyer's review of the contract and of the laws in the places it will be published. Never a celebrity, a public figure, or any real person who has not signed. Never imitating a living artist's recognisable voice or style to sell as theirs. Laws on voice, likeness, copyright and AI labelling differ by country and change; none of this is legal advice.

## Economics

Every piece records cost (tokens, model, seconds, dollars) and later revenue as trail frames, so the economic gates can flag a piece or channel that is not earning. Gates flag; they never act on their own.

## OPEN
