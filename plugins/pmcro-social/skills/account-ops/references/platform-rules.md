# Platform rules for account work

Status: company rules (ADR 0018). Not legal advice. Each platform's automation, AI-label and affiliate rules differ and change; none were fetched here. Before drafting for a platform, open its current policy pages and note the date you checked.

## Always

- One real identity per account: the founder's or the company's, stated plainly.
- Label AI-generated or synthetic voice and images the way the platform requires, and in the content itself (ADR 0008).
- Disclose affiliate links and paid relationships next to the link (see `pmcro-cloudflare`).
- Use the platform's official tools or API for anything automated, with the human's approval; never scrape logins or share sessions.

## Never

- Fake accounts, purchased followers, coordinated fake engagement, or impersonation of another person or brand.
- Giving an agent a password or session cookie.

## Pace

Prefer fewer accounts posted consistently over many neglected ones. A cadence in the registry is a promise to yourself; if it is not kept, change the cadence or retire the account.
