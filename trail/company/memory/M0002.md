Search results on supervised fine-tuning: quality beats volume. LIMA reports strong results from about 1,000 curated examples; guides say about 500 good examples for style and format, 1,000 to 5,000 for domain specialization, diminishing returns after that. For PMCR-O the limit is the number of examples an independent Dataset Checker has cleared, not the number generated. Owner exports are candidates, not verified trails, and private content stays local. See docs/product/training-data-spec.md and competitors-and-models.md.
